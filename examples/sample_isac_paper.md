# Synthetic ISAC Study

This synthetic study considers integrated sensing and communication with joint beamforming. A fully integrated
MIMO-OFDM transmitter serves communication users and performs monostatic sensing with the same waveform and total
transmit-power budget.

The simulation jointly optimizes achievable communication rate and the sensing Cramer-Rao bound (CRB). It assumes
perfect channel state information and a point-target model. The study reports no hardware-in-the-loop, SDR,
over-the-air, or real-world dataset evidence.

This file is a public synthetic fixture. It does not describe a real paper or establish real-world performance.
